## 2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only what the service requires, and nothing beyond the list below unless we tell you first.

**Customer contacts**

Name, phone number, email address, billing email address, postal address.

**Sales agents**

Name, phone number, email address, postal address, tax number (NPWP), national identity number (KTP), bank name, account number, account holder name, and commission rate.

KTP and bank details are **specific personal data** (financial data) under article 4 of UU PDP, and we handle them more strictly than general data.

**Staff**

Name, email address (login account), role, and account status. Passwords are held as hashes by the authentication service; the Company cannot see the originals.

**Service operations data**

Credentials for customer systems entrusted to us, IP phone extension details (user name, extension number), and incident and inspection records.

We **do not collect** biometric data, genetic data, health data, criminal records, children's data, location data, or advertising identifiers.

## 6. Transfers Outside Indonesia

Because the processors above sit outside Indonesia, personal data is transferred abroad. Under article 56 of UU PDP, we bind each processor by a data processing agreement incorporating standard contractual clauses, so that the level of protection at the receiving end matches what this law requires.

## 7. Security Measures

- Managed system passwords are stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and the encryption key is held separately from the database. A leak of the database alone cannot recover them.
- All traffic is carried over TLS.
- Access is limited by role. A sales agent's account can see only their own customers and contracts, and the database enforces this directly.
- For lower-privileged roles, bank account and tax numbers are shown only in part.
- Administrative actions and every export of personal data — printed documents, exported histories, password decryption — are recorded, and those records cannot be altered or deleted.

## 8. Your Rights

Under articles 5 to 15 of UU PDP, you may:

- **Access and obtain a copy** of the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** data that is inaccurate
- **Erase** data whose purpose has been served
- **Withdraw consent** where processing rests on consent
- **Restrict processing and object** to specific processing
- **Receive your data in a machine-readable format** (portability)
- **Claim compensation** for loss caused by a breach of the law

Write to [[privacy contact email]]. We will verify your identity, act on the request, and tell you the outcome. Erasure may be limited for data we are required to retain under tax law; where that applies, we will explain why.

## 9. If Personal Data Is Breached

We notify affected individuals and the supervisory authority within **3x24 hours** of becoming aware of a breach. The notice states what kinds of data were exposed, when and how it happened, the likely impact, what the Company has done, what you can do, and who to contact.
